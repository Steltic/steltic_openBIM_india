## PEB envelope, restraint and fabrication review, IN_Ex1–5

- [ ] **Coordinate structural and architectural height datums.** The portals follow the supplied eave and ridge worklines. Packing raises the physical Z members and sheet above their supporting flanges. The Bengaluru inner roof sheet is 850.2 mm above the structural datum, Nagpur 739.7 mm and Farrukhnagar 845.2 mm. Bhiwandi's structural ridge is 15,400 mm. The lowest modeled main-rafter soffit is 12,525.6 mm and the dock canopy soffit is 5,500 mm. Confirm surveyed steel, service hangers and overhangs before setting rack or door heights.
- [ ] **Check each real bearing and fly-brace attachment.** The export includes separate steel packing and measured Z-flange contact on the roof and façades. The independent IFC check measured contact at all 10,501 interfaces across the five jobs. Bhiwandi alone has 6,337 interfaces with at least 422.4 mm² common area. The five jobs contain 2,310 full flange braces. Their minimum distances to the roof and long-wall sheet faces are 229.85 and 242.36 mm. The Bengaluru retrofit retains the existing steel. Its longest brace measures 1,499.9 mm against the native 1,500 mm limit. Verify the supplier's cleats, uplift, local Z-flange resistance, lap lengths, third-point bridging and cladding fasteners.
- [ ] **Place shutters clear of every primary member and projecting anchor.** Nagpur's two 6 × 6 m gable openings required cranked wind posts. The roof centres remain at 6 m. The lower posts shift 800 mm below 6.8 m, leaving 52 mm between the clear opening and adjacent anchor shanks. Check the full-strength shop change of direction and roller-shutter drives. Farrukhnagar's ten 3 × 3.5 m dock doors occupy unbraced bays. In Bhiwandi, the four 6 × 6 m gable doors sit between the 8 m wind posts and the twenty 3 × 3.5 m side doors are distributed around the SCBF braces. Confirm dock-leveller pits and manufacturer reactions with the foundation engineer.
- [ ] **Keep the specified daylight area and supplier weights in the envelope design.** Bengaluru, Bhiwandi and Farrukhnagar each have separate translucent sheets in openings covering 3% of the main roof's plan area. The Bengaluru retrofit keeps those sheets clear of its four PV fields, which cover 75% of plan and sit 100 mm clear of the outer sheet. The dead-load takeoff includes 10% allowance for secondary laps and sheet ribs. Bhiwandi and Farrukhnagar include 50 mm glass-wool insulation. Ridge vents, weathering, opening trims, masonry ties and plinth supports require supplier or foundation details.
- [ ] Detail the selected 80 mm local column flange and its transition, panel web reinforcement, stiffeners, continuity plates and CJP preparations. End-plate and double-fin dimensions and the separate moment and shear bolt groups are included in the candidate drawings. Verify the shear load path across the mating interface, host drilling and metal remaining around the openings for fabrication.
